Control4 Contemporary

The flexible everyday choice

Contemporary keypads replace a group of ordinary switches with clear, engraved actions. The buttons can control lighting scenes, shades, music and other Control4 features.

  • Multiple button layouts
  • Backlit custom engraving
  • Several colors and finishes
  • Works well in both finished homes and new construction
Explore Contemporary buttons and colors →
Lux by Control4

The architectural choice

Lux is designed for spaces where the keypad should feel like part of the interior. Metal finishes, clean edges and engraved buttons make it a strong fit for kitchens, primary suites and formal rooms.

  • Premium metal and designer finishes
  • Clean magnetic faceplates
  • Wired and wireless choices
  • Coordinates with other Lux wall devices

Two useful supporting controls

Not every wall needs a full engraved keypad. These simpler options can make the overall plan cleaner and more practical.

Lutron Pico

Flexible scene control

Pico can be used on a wall, on a tabletop pedestal or as a handheld control. It is useful when a convenient extra control location is needed.

Before the keypads are ordered

Once the visual direction is selected, we confirm the details that make the finished system work properly.

1

Choose the finish

Compare it with paint, stone, cabinetry and nearby hardware.

2

Choose the actions

Keep the most-used scenes on the wall and leave rare adjustments in the app.

3

Confirm the design

We verify wiring, lighting loads, device type and system compatibility.

One important detail: some models are scene controls only, while others also dim the light connected at that wall. They can look similar from the front, so we confirm the correct version during design.
