Control4 T5 vs T4: which touchscreen fits your room?

Compare the screen sizes, see how wall and tabletop models look, and choose a location that feels natural to use.

Published July 12, 2026 Updated: October 19, 2026By Denali Tech Team6 min read

The short answer: T5 is the newer in-wall family, in 8-inch and 11-inch sizes. T4 comes in 8-inch and 10-inch sizes, with separate in-wall and tabletop models. Choose the mounting style first, then the screen size you can comfortably use.

Both families need a configured Control4 system; neither is a standalone smart-home hub. The controls available on screen depend on your connected equipment and programming.

T5 and T4: the differences that matter

A screen near the kitchen entrance serves a different purpose from one beside the bed. Start with where and how you want to use it.

Newer in-wall family

Control4 T5

A wall-mounted interface with magnetic trim and a proximity sensor.

  • 8-inch or 11-inch; in-wall only.
  • Coordinated Lux trim options, including a paintable Primer finish.
  • Built-in Power over Ethernet (PoE) brings power and a wired network connection over one cable.
  • Requires Control4 X4.2 / OS 4.2.0 or later. Check your controller and upgrade path before ordering.
Wall or tabletop

Control4 T4

An established family with wall-mounted and portable tabletop versions.

  • 8-inch or 10-inch in each mounting style.
  • Tabletop models use Wi-Fi and include a powered charging base with magnetic docking.
  • In-wall models support Ethernet or Wi-Fi with the appropriate PoE or AC power hardware.
  • A working T4 may still meet your needs. Review compatibility and the reason for upgrading before replacing it.

Replacing a 7-inch screen? It belongs to an earlier Control4 generation, not T4 or T5. Identify the existing model, wall box and wiring before choosing a replacement.

All four sizes have a 1920 × 1200 display. A larger diagonal gives you more physical screen area, not a higher pixel count. Compare them from the distance where you will actually stand or sit.

Want a screen beside the bed or on a desk?

A tabletop T4 is a separate model—not an in-wall screen placed on a stand. Plan a convenient power outlet for its charging base and reliable Wi-Fi where you will use it.

What needs to work behind the screen?

The display, wall box, power, network and software must work together. For an upgrade, inspect what is already there before opening the wall or ordering parts.

See the trim, wall-box and power details

T5 trim

A trim is pre-installed. Optional Lux-coordinated finishes and a paintable Primer trim let you match nearby controls or the wall.

T5 mounting and power

The wall bracket and screw kit are included. T5 has built-in PoE; the optional C4-T5IW-AC accessory is for supported AC-powered installations.

T5 wall box

Use a standard US two-gang box or a listed Control4 box as specified in the guide. Control4-branded plastic wall boxes are not compatible with the optional AC adapter. Have an electrician handle mains-power work.

T4 in-wall hardware

The matching power/mid box and approved wall box matter. T4 power and mounting parts are not interchangeable with T5 parts; identify the existing hardware first.

T4 tabletop base

A charging base is included. Replacement bases are size-specific; use the documented permanent-mount kit if the screen needs to stay in place.

Network and handover

Use a wired connection for an in-wall screen where practical. Test Wi-Fi at the intended tabletop location and check everyday controls and any intercom functions before handover.

Prewire checklist before drywall closes

  • Confirm the exact screen model and mounting style.
  • Choose a height and location the household can comfortably reach.
  • Plan Ethernet and compatible PoE for an in-wall screen.
  • Match the wall box, power hardware and bracket to the model.
  • Coordinate trim with nearby controls and finishes.
  • Keep the location clear of heat, moisture and extended direct sunlight.
  • Leave a convenient outlet for a tabletop charging base.
  • Confirm controller, software and required integrations before ordering.

A simple room-by-room recommendation

For a new wall location, start by comparing the T5 8-inch and 11-inch at your normal viewing distance. For a bedside table or desk, consider the T4 tabletop instead.

If your T4 already works well, keep it unless there is a clear reason to change—such as mounting style, readability or a feature you need. Check the controller and wiring before deciding on an upgrade.
