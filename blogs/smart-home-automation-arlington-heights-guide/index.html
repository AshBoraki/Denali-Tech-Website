Popular Smart Home Features in Arlington Heights

These are common project categories worth comparing:

1. Smart Lighting Control

Smart lighting can provide the following functions when the selected controls, loads, interfaces, and integrations support them:

  • Control selected lights from supported phones or voice platforms
  • Create scenes—for example, a reviewed Goodnight scene that turns off selected lights and requests compatible lock actions
  • Automate based on time of day or motion sensors
  • Reduce unnecessary lighting use with appropriately configured schedules or occupancy sensing
  • Run planned occupancy-lighting routines without representing them as a substitute for a security system

Lighting option: Lutron offers Caséta, RadioRA 3, and HomeWorks QSX for different project scales. Compatibility with Control4, URC, fixtures, and voice platforms is system- and model-specific.

Budget from: The actual load count and type, control and keypad plan, system platform, finishes, fixture compatibility, electrical work, programming, and integration.

2. Security & Surveillance Systems

Security is a top priority for Arlington Heights families. Smart security systems include:

  • 4K security cameras (indoor and outdoor)
  • Video doorbells (Ring, Nest, or integrated systems)
  • Smart locks (keyless entry, remote locking)
  • Motion sensors and door/window sensors
  • Professional monitoring and mobile app access

Why it matters in Arlington Heights: While Arlington Heights is a safe community, smart security provides peace of mind, especially when traveling or at work. Many Arlington Heights homeowners value being able to check their home from anywhere.

Budget from: Camera locations and models, lenses and low-light goals, cable routes, recorder and retention, network capacity, remote access, monitoring, and privacy requirements.

3. Motorized Shades & Window Treatments

Motorized shades are incredibly popular in Arlington Heights homes, especially for:

  • Large windows in living rooms and great rooms
  • Bedrooms for automated wake-up routines
  • Energy efficiency (close during hot summer days, open for natural light)
  • Privacy control with a button press
  • Integration with smart home scenes

Popular brands in Arlington Heights: Hunter Douglas and Lutron motorized shades are the most requested. They're reliable, quiet, and integrate reliably with Control4 and URC systems.

Budget from: Field-measured window sizes, shade type, fabric, power and wiring, fascia or pocket details, controls, installation access, and integration.

4. Whole Home Audio

Music throughout your Arlington Heights home is a game-changer. Whole home audio includes:

  • In-wall or in-ceiling speakers in multiple rooms
  • Sonos systems or distributed audio
  • Control from phone, tablet, or voice
  • Different music in different rooms, or synchronized throughout
  • Outdoor speakers for patios and decks

Popular in Arlington Heights: Sonos systems are very popular for their ease of use and wireless capabilities. For higher-end installations, distributed audio systems provide professional-grade sound throughout the home.

Budget from: Zone count, speaker and subwoofer selection, amplification, sources, wiring, rack equipment, outdoor areas, control, and commissioning.

5. Home Theater Systems

Arlington Heights homeowners love creating dedicated entertainment spaces. Home theaters include:

  • 4K laser projectors or large-screen TVs
  • Dolby Atmos surround sound systems
  • Acoustic treatment for optimal sound
  • Smart lighting control (dim lights when movie starts)
  • Comfortable seating and room design

Useful for: Basements, bonus rooms, or dedicated media rooms in Arlington Heights homes. Many Arlington Heights homeowners convert unused space into impressive home theaters.

Budget from: Room construction, display or projection, screen, speakers, subwoofers, electronics, acoustics, seating, lighting, control, calibration, and finish work.

6. WiFi & Network Solutions

Strong WiFi is essential for smart homes. Arlington Heights homes often need:

  • Mesh WiFi systems for whole-home coverage
  • WiFi 6 or WiFi 6E for faster speeds
  • Measured coverage and performance in the agreed rooms and outdoor areas
  • Capacity planned from client types, traffic, wired links, radios, and applications

Why it matters: Multiple floors and dense building materials can weaken radio signals. A survey helps decide whether better placement, wired access points, managed mesh, cabling, or equipment changes are appropriate.

Budget from: Survey, router and switch requirements, access-point count and model, PoE, cabling, rack work, configuration, migration, and post-install testing.

Arlington Heights Smart Home Considerations

Arlington Heights has some unique characteristics that smart home installers should consider:

Older Home Wiring

Many Arlington Heights homes are older (built before 1980), which can mean:

  • May need updated electrical work for smart switches
  • Walls may need strategic access for wiring
  • Older construction can be more challenging to wire

Planning note: Modern smart homes often combine wired and wireless systems. Existing wiring may be reusable, but access points, cameras, touchscreens, distributed audio, shades, lighting panels, door stations, and racks can require new power, low-voltage cable, or electrical work.

Chicago-Area Weather

Arlington Heights experiences all four seasons, which means:

  • Outdoor security cameras need to handle Chicago winters and summers
  • Compatible thermostat schedules and setbacks may reduce unnecessary heating or cooling; results depend on the home, HVAC system, weather, rates, and previous use
  • Appropriately selected shades can change solar gain, glare, privacy, and comfort, but the effect depends on glazing, fabric, exposure, and how they are operated

Exterior equipment should be selected by its published temperature, moisture, enclosure, mounting, power, and service requirements for the actual exposed location.

Home Sizes

Arlington Heights has a wide range of home sizes, from 1,500 sq ft starter homes to 5,000+ sq ft estates. Smart home systems scale to any size home - we design systems that fit your specific Arlington Heights home.
