Understand the Lux family

The front can look beautifully consistent while the device behind it performs a different job. These are the four useful categories to understand.

01

Configurable keypad

Dedicated buttons for scenes and whole-home actions such as Welcome, Away, Music or Goodnight.

02

Keypad dimmer

Controls the light connected at that wall and adds buttons for scenes or other rooms.

03

Keypad switch

Provides local on/off control plus configurable buttons for additional actions.

04

Wired keypad

A dedicated control point for projects planned around prewire or centralized lighting.

Why this matters: two Lux keypads may look almost identical from the front but serve different purposes. Final device selection comes after the room and electrical plan are reviewed.

Wired or wireless?

Both can deliver the same calm, coordinated appearance. The right path usually depends on whether the walls are open and how the lighting system is designed.

New build or remodel

Wired Lux keypad

Best considered while the wiring and lighting-panel plan can still be coordinated.

  • Clean dedicated control locations
  • Works naturally with centralized lighting plans
  • Button functions can be planned room by room
Finished home

Wireless Lux device

A practical way to bring the Lux look into an existing home without redesigning every wall.

  • Useful for upgrades and renovations
  • Can replace selected existing controls
  • Device type is matched to the load and location

See the finish before you choose

The keypad should relate to the paint, stone, cabinetry and nearby metal hardware. Compare samples in the actual room whenever possible.

Plan the labels around real routines

Short, familiar labels are easier to understand than technical circuit names. A visitor should be able to use the room without an explanation.
