Control4 ownership guide

What does Control4 Connect actually do?

Connect adds features such as away-from-home app access and notifications. On X4, it is an optional subscription—not a fee just to use your system locally. Older OS 3 accounts need a different check.

Published August 17, 2026 Updated: October 19, 2026By Denali Tech Team6 min read

Short answer: On X4, you can keep local app control without Connect. The subscription adds remote access and other premium features. Before cancelling an older plan, check whether your system runs X4 or OS 3 and whether the account uses Connect or 4Sight.

What Connect adds to everyday use

The useful question is which features you actually use. Control4 lists the following benefits; compatible devices, software, account setup and internet service are still needed. Manufacturer feature list.

App control away from home

Use supported home controls when your phone is no longer on the home network.

Notifications

Receive configured alerts. A leak or door alert also needs the appropriate sensor and programming; the subscription alone cannot detect it.

Mobile intercom

Use Intercom Anywhere with supported door stations, doorbells and touchscreens. It does not make every camera an intercom.

HomeKit and voice

Access supported Apple HomeKit and voice integrations. Device compatibility and setup determine which controls are available.

Personalization

Use supported Routines and When >> Then tools to adjust selected automations. These do not replace professional system programming.

Backups and insights

Connect includes system-backup and insight features, plus access to new premium functionality. A backup is not a promise of free repair or replacement.

X4, OS 3 or 4Sight: check before changing a plan

The original April 23, 2024 cutoff explained which accounts used 4Sight or Connect. It does not tell the whole story today: the later X4 policy made Connect optional. Your software version and existing subscription now matter too.

X4 systems

Local use without Connect

Control4's optional-Connect policy keeps local app access on X4. It also lists local Apple Watch use, Apple Music integration and AirPlay 2 among retained features, subject to compatible hardware. Apple Music itself may require a separate music subscription.

Remote app access and Connect's premium features are separate. Check your renewal screen before changing the subscription. Read the X4 policy source.

Earlier accounts

Do not apply X4 rules to every OS 3 home

The U.S. FAQ warns that OS 3 Connect accounts can lose local app access when Connect expires. That is different from the X4 policy.

Eligible existing 4Sight customers who stay on OS 3 can continue renewing 4Sight. Confirm your actual account before cancelling, changing plans or scheduling an upgrade. Read the U.S. FAQ.

Local does not mean everything works without internet. A working controller, home network and connected devices are still needed. Streaming services and other cloud-connected products can have their own internet or subscription requirements.

What does Connect cost in the U.S.?

Control4's published U.S. policy lists $249 per year, including projects with multiple controllers. That is a published reference price, not a quote for your account; confirm the current charge, tax, term and renewal settings in the Control4 app.

The transition policy says existing $249 X4 plans become optional at renewal. It also extends specified $0/$99 CA-1 or CORE Lite X4 plans at $0 through the end of 2026—not indefinitely. Official November 12, 2025 announcement.

Do not cancel a plan or update the system just to follow a general price guide. First check the account-specific offer and the features you want to keep.

The app and Watch: the connection matters

A familiar screen can be used at home or away, but the included features differ. X4 retains local Watch use without Connect; remote Watch use is a Connect feature. U.S. feature comparison.

When the problem is not the subscription

If a physical remote, light or room stops responding, check the scope of the fault before buying a plan. Connect cannot repair failed hardware, weak Wi-Fi, incorrect wiring or programming. Conversely, if local control works but away-from-home access does not, subscription status is one of the things to check—not the only possible cause.

There is no need to replace working equipment merely because the service name changed. An upgrade should follow a compatibility review and a clear reason for the change.

Before activating, renewing or cancelling

1. Check the account

Confirm the homeowner email, registered system, plan name and renewal date. Do not send your password with a service request.

2. Identify the software

Record the controller model and whether the system runs OS 3 or X4. Do not assume an old system supports every new feature.

3. Name the features you use

List app access at home and away, alerts, intercom and voice. Ask what would change on your specific account.

4. Check the full cost

Separate the manufacturer subscription from any music service, upgrade work or integrator support agreement.

Connect is not an onsite service plan

Control4 Assist is a separate manufacturer-backed remote-support offering. Denali Tech or another integrator may also provide separately scoped service. Do not assume Connect includes visits, replacement hardware, cabling repairs or all programming work. Ask for the included work and any additional charges in writing.
