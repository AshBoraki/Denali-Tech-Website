First, choose what the device needs to do

The correct device depends on whether that wall controls a local light, serves only as a command point, or belongs to a centralized lighting plan.

Finished homes

Wireless keypad dimmer

Controls the light connected at that wall and adds configurable buttons for scenes and other Control4 actions.

New build or remodel

Wired keypad

A dedicated command point for lights, scenes, music, security and other functions in a planned Control4 system.

Simple locations

Dimmer or switch

Keeps familiar local control in rooms that do not need several engraved scene buttons.

Important: a keypad dimmer and a wired keypad can look similar from the front. Denali Tech confirms the wiring, connected load and system design before the final device is ordered.

A keypad can control more than lighting

The best layouts place frequent whole-room actions on the wall and leave occasional adjustments in the app or touchscreen.

Lighting and shadesSet the room brightness and shade position together.
Music and televisionStart a familiar entertainment scene without searching for a remote.
Welcome and AwayCoordinate lights, selected locks, climate and security routines.
GoodnightTurn off selected rooms and prepare the home for the night.
Gate or garagePlace an important arrival action at a convenient wall.
Status feedbackProgrammable LEDs can show whether an assigned action is active.

Use labels everyone understands

Keep the engraving short and describe the result, not the electrical circuit. Control4 recommends concise text because each button has limited space.
