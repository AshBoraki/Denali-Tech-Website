WattBox 900: power protection built for a serviceable smart home

The equipment rack is where the network, automation, cameras and entertainment systems meet. WattBox 900 adds long-life surge protection, outlet-by-outlet visibility, and a practical way for an authorized technician to diagnose and recover selected equipment remotely.

The short answer: WattBox 900 is a strong fit when a home has a central AV or network rack that must stay protected, observable and easy to service. It does not replace a UPS, a generator or a properly designed electrical circuit.

First, understand what you are buying

This is not a glamorous device you touch every day. Its value appears when equipment locks up, power becomes unstable or a technician needs to diagnose the rack without making an unnecessary trip.

What it is

A professional IP power conditioner with surge protection, individual outlet control, power metering and OvrC remote management.

What it is not

It is not battery backup by itself. A compatible WattBox UPS is added when the system needs runtime during an outage.

Why it matters at home

The rack can report abnormal power, restart one frozen component and give the service team better information before a visit.

The hardware

One unit gives every device a known place

Each connected component can be named in OvrC—router, network switch, Control4 controller, camera recorder or streaming player. A technician can then see what is drawing power and restart the correct outlet without interrupting the whole rack.

Protection that stays on duty

What SEC changes

Traditional surge components can wear as they absorb electrical events. The 900 Series combines non-sacrificial Surge Elimination Circuit protection on line-to-neutral with additional MOV protection modes. The goal is consistent protection over the life of the installed system—not a power strip that quietly becomes a weak link.

This still does not make the rack immune to every electrical problem. Grounding, circuit quality, installation and the rest of the power plan remain important.

What better service looks like day to day

The most useful WattBox 900 features happen in software. The system records behavior, alerts the service team and makes a controlled reset possible without asking a homeowner to find the correct plug.

What happens when the utility power goes out?

Pairing the 900 Series with a compatible OvrC UPS adds battery health alerts, on-battery notifications, remote alarm silencing and smart load shedding. The system can keep the modem, router, switch and automation controller online while shutting down less important loads first.

  • Battery runtime is calculated from the real connected wattage
  • Critical and noncritical outlets are identified before commissioning
  • A UPS bridges short outages; it does not replace a generator for long events

Is the 900 Series right for your project?

It makes sense when…

  • You have a central rack with network, control, surveillance or AV equipment
  • You want Denali to diagnose and restart supported devices remotely
  • Long-life surge protection and a six-year warranty matter
  • You want outlet-level power history instead of simple on/off control

Choose another path when…

  • You only need power for one TV or a very small cabinet
  • The system requires a true 20A design
  • Your main requirement is battery runtime rather than managed power
  • The rack plan cannot provide 2U plus appropriate cable and service space

Questions homeowners ask

Is WattBox 900 a UPS?

No. It provides managed power, protection, metering and remote outlet control. A compatible UPS is a separate component that adds temporary battery runtime.

Can I restart equipment myself?

Denali can provide selected device resets and approved macros in the OvrC Connect customer app. Critical equipment should stay protected from accidental resets.

How much rack space does it need?

The WB-900-SEC-IPVM-12 is officially listed as 2U, approximately 17.32 inches wide, 3.46 inches high and 8.39 inches deep. The rack also needs sensible cable routing, ventilation and service access.

Does it work with Control4 or URC?

The 900 Series supports IP and Telnet APIs and is designed to integrate with professional control platforms including Control4 and URC. The final driver and programming scope are confirmed during system design.
