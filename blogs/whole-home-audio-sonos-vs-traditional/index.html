Whole-home audio comparison

Sonos whole-house audio or a wired system?

Start with the rooms, not the brand. Sonos can use freestanding speakers or power wired ceiling speakers. A central audio system can use Sonos sources too. The right design depends on how you want to listen and what you want to see.

Published January 11, 2025 Updated: October 19, 2026By Denali Tech Team7 min read

Short answer: Consider self-powered speakers when flexible placement and fewer cable routes matter. Consider wired architectural speakers when a discreet, permanent installation matters. Either can use a Sonos playback platform. Neither design guarantees better sound, lower cost or freedom from network problems.

Count listening zones, not just speakers

A zone is an area you want to control independently. A kitchen and dining room can be one zone if they should always share music and volume—or separate zones if they should not.

Speakers

Coverage comes from the speaker layout. Adding a second pair does not automatically create another independent music zone.

Amplifier channels

Channels power passive speakers. Stereo normally uses a left and right channel; channel count is not the same as independent source count.

Sources and streams

Different songs at the same time require enough independent playback streams or source paths. Streaming-account rules matter too.

For example, a kitchen, bedroom and patio that each need their own music and volume require three independent listening zones. Write that down before choosing the amplifier or counting ceiling grilles.

Sonos is a platform, not one speaker style

A self-powered model such as Era 100 keeps the speaker and amplification together. Sonos Amp instead powers compatible passive speakers over speaker cable. Port adds Sonos playback to a compatible amplifier or receiver; Port does not power passive speakers directly. Amp guide · Port guide.

Sonos can be a practical way to start with one room and add compatible rooms later. Before adding to an older system, check app-generation compatibility and the current requirements of each player—not just the logo on the box.

What a centrally amplified wired system changes

Here, passive speakers connect by speaker cable to equipment in a rack or cabinet. Streamers provide the music, a matrix may route sources, amplifiers power the speakers, and a control system may coordinate operation. Some products combine several of these jobs.

The benefit is a planned installation with less equipment in the room and access to a broad speaker selection. The trade-off is cable routing, fixed speaker positions, equipment space and service access. Grilles are usually still visible; “architectural” does not automatically mean invisible.

Wired speakers do not make streaming offline. Speaker cable carries amplified audio from the amplifier. Streaming players, apps and control systems can still depend on the home network, internet access and service accounts. Wired Sonos networking is also a different question from wired speaker connections. Sonos network guidance.

Compare quotes using the same room list

There is no useful universal “six-room price” without knowing what those rooms need. Ask each proposal to cover the same:

  • Zones and sources: which rooms play together and how many different streams must play at once.
  • Speakers and output: exact models, placement, subwoofers and the intended listening level.
  • Installation: power, cable routes, mounts, backboxes, rack space, ventilation and finish repairs.
  • Controls and handoff: app setup, service accounts, programming, tuning, testing and documentation.
  • Ongoing costs: relevant subscriptions, support scope and exclusions.

For existing ceiling speakers, identify the models, wiring, condition and amplifier load before promising reuse. Extra speakers cannot simply be connected until the amplifier's load requirements are checked. Useful equipment should stay when it suits the plan.

Choose around the home you have

Finished home, few cable routes

Self-powered speakers may avoid major wall work. Check outlet positions, coverage and where the speakers can sit before buying.

New build or remodel

Plan architectural-speaker cable while access is open. Sonos amplification or another central platform can be selected around the same room plan.

Working system already installed

Inspect it first. A compatible streamer, amplifier change or network repair may be enough; a full replacement is not the starting assumption.

A hybrid can make sense: wired ceiling speakers in the kitchen and a self-powered player in an office, for example. A shared Sonos platform can group compatible rooms. Mixing unrelated playback platforms does not automatically give synchronized whole-home music.

Control4, URC and TV audio: check the actual workflow

Do not choose an audio system on the promise that “everything is integrated.” For a Control4 or URC project, ask for a demonstration of source selection, favorites, grouping and volume with the proposed products and current driver or module. Some setup and service functions may still use the Sonos app.

URC documents Sonos integration specifically with Total Control; that is not a promise that every URC handheld remote offers the same browsing features. URC's integration announcement.

Will TV sound stay in sync in other rooms?

Music grouping is not the same as a home-theater surround connection. Sonos documents a group-audio delay when TV sound is sent to grouped non-home-theater rooms. Test lip-sync and any audible echo in adjacent rooms before promising a TV-audio design. Sonos TV-grouping guidance.

Does Ethernet fix every Sonos problem?

No. Ethernet can help supported devices, but the exact model and network design matter. Sonos lists Era 100, Era 300 and other products that do not use its dedicated SonosNet wireless network. Do not apply an old “wire one player” recipe to every current system. Check Sonos's current exceptions.

Can the same system include outdoor speakers?

Yes, with speakers, cable and mounting suited to the actual exposure. Keep indoor-only amplifiers and players in a location permitted by their instructions. A covered patio is not automatically a dry indoor environment.
