URC FAQ

What to expect from a local URC dealer.

Do you install new URC systems?

Yes. We design and install URC for a single media room or a connected whole home, then tailor the controls to your household.

Can you help with a system another dealer installed?

Yes. We first review what is present and working, then explain whether service, reprogramming, replacement, or an upgrade offers the best value.

Will you show us how everything works?

Yes. A personal walkthrough is part of a thoughtful installation, and ongoing service is available when the home changes later.

Tell us what you want the home to do.

Planning something new? Tell us about the rooms and the experience you want. Need service? A few photos of the remote, equipment, and problem will help us understand the next step.
